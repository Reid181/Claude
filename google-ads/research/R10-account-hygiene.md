# R10: Google Ads account hygiene and defaults for a new or dormant account (Oct 2026)

Prepared 15 Oct 2026 for Endless Parks (Perth, AUD, A$30/day). Builds on R01; does not repeat campaign architecture.

Method note: 28 extended web searches. Direct page fetches to support.google.com, searchengineland.com, ppc.land, help.shopify.com and almcorp.com were refused by this session's network policy, so quotes below come from search extracts of those pages rather than full-page reads. Confidence is marked down where a fact rests on a single secondary source. Anything marked "verify in UI" should be checked on screen when Jake signs in.

## 1. Findings

1. **Auto-apply recommendations: nothing is safe to leave ticked, and there is no master switch.** Path: Campaigns > Recommendations > Auto-apply (top right) > Manage tab. Google groups the options into two bundles, "Maintain your ads" and "Grow your business". Untick every box in both and save. The highest-damage ones for a small Shopping plus Search account are: Add broad match keywords, Add AI Max, Set a target CPA / target ROAS, Raise your budgets, Expand reach with search partners, Add Display expansion, Use Performance Max, Add automatically created assets, Remove redundant / conflicting negative keywords, Use optimised ad rotation. Dismissing a recommendation gives the same optimisation score uplift as applying it, so there is no score penalty for saying no. Sources: Google Ads Help 10276359 (accessed 15 Oct 2026); Search Engine Land "The truth about Google Ads recommendations (and auto-apply)". Confidence: High on path and the no-master-switch point, Med on exact recommendation names (Google renames them often).

2. **New since 12 Oct 2026: Automated Promotions scrape coupons from your website and attach them to Search and PMax ads, opt-out only.** Google's 2 Sep 2026 notice said it would "automatically identify and extract high-quality promotions, such as coupons and deals, from your website" from 12 Oct 2026 for eligible Search and PMax campaigns. Early reporting said eligibility was limited to campaigns with linked location assets and no manual promotion assets; later reporting of Google's email says advertisers are being opted in broadly. Opt out: Campaigns > Assets > More (three dots) > Account-level automated assets > More > Advanced settings > Automated promotions > Off. This matters directly for Jake's "no sale hooks in evergreen" rule: the 10% welcome popup on endlessparks.com is exactly the kind of on-page offer this feature would lift into ad copy. Sources: ppc.land 2 Sep 2026; Search Engine Roundtable 1 Oct 2026; Common Thread Collective Oct 2026. Confidence: High that the feature is live and opt-out, Med on the eligibility detail (sources disagree).

3. **Account-level automated assets are all on by default; campaign-level settings override account-level.** Same path as finding 2. Toggles listed in 2025 to 2026 guides: Dynamic sitelinks, Dynamic callouts, Dynamic structured snippets, Seller ratings, Longer ad headlines, Automated apps, Automated locations, Dynamic images, and now Automated promotions. Google's own help text for PMax (answer 16049135) says campaigns "default to the account-level setting" but can override per campaign, and one guide warns that campaign-level on beats account-level off, so check each campaign after creation. Recommended for Endless Parks: keep Seller ratings on (Judge.me is a Google-licensed review partner and the store is at 99 reviews, near the 100-review threshold for the rating to show), keep Dynamic sitelinks on until manual sitelinks exist, turn off Dynamic images, Longer ad headlines, Dynamic callouts, Dynamic structured snippets, Automated promotions. Sources: Google Ads Help 7175034 and 16049135; Bronco; Practical Pig Consulting Jan 2026; Olivia Lawson. Confidence: High on path and on-by-default, Med on the exact toggle list.

4. **AI Max for Search is on by default for every new Search campaign, and existing campaigns were auto-upgraded from 1 Sep 2026.** Google Ads Help 15909989 states new Search campaigns have AI Max turned on by default; it is pre-selected in the campaign creation flow and can be switched off there or later at Campaigns > (campaign) > Settings > AI Max. The September 2026 wave upgraded campaigns that used automatically created assets (search term matching and text customisation both on) or the campaign-level broad match setting (search term matching only). Dynamic Search Ads were deferred to February 2027. Practical consequence for a new account: there is no longer a separate "broad match keywords" campaign toggle to worry about, but every new Search campaign needs AI Max toggled off at step one, and the old trap (broad match default) now lives inside AI Max's search term matching. Sources: Google Ads Help 15909989; Google blog "Dynamic Search Ads are upgrading to AI Max" (2026); Common Thread Collective Aug 2026; overads.io. Confidence: High.

5. **Channel prioritisation and budget allocation launched 14 Oct 2026, rolling out from 15 Oct 2026 over several months, and it replaces the old network checkboxes over time.** Set at account, shared budget or campaign level: Admin > Account settings > Channel prioritisation card > pick campaign type > set each channel High or Low, with an optional daily percentage cap on Low channels. Defaults: every channel is High. Channel groupings: Search = Google Search, search partners, Display Network; Shopping = Google Search, search partners; PMax = Google channels (Search, YouTube, Maps, Gmail, Discover as one block), search partners, Display Network; Demand Gen gets priority only, no budget allocation. Caps are "best effort", not hard. Search Engine Land and ppc.land report 0% is allowed and effectively switches a channel off; the Help page extract did not confirm 0%, so verify in UI. From February 2027 the current spend allocation for Search campaigns with Display turned on goes away; to keep today's behaviour set Display to Low at 50%. It cannot be set from an MCC. Shopping shared budgets only work if every campaign in the budget is Shopping. Sources: Google Ads Help 18385152; Google blog 14 Oct 2026; Search Engine Land 14 Oct 2026; Search Engine Journal; ppc.land; MediaPost 14 Oct 2026. Confidence: High on launch and structure, Med on 0% and on whether Jake's account has it yet (progressive rollout).

6. **Search campaign creation defaults still include search partners and Display Network ticked, and location set to "Presence or interest".** Networks: during creation, untick "Include Google search partners" and "Include Google Display Network" (or, if the campaign shows the new channel card, set both Low with 0% if allowed). Location: Settings > Locations > Location options > choose "Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations" instead of the recommended default "Presence or interest: People in, regularly in, or who've shown interest in your targeted locations". Ad rotation: Settings > Additional settings > Ad rotation, default is "Optimise: Prefer best performing ads", which is fine and required for Smart Bidding; "Do not optimise" still exists for Manual CPC testing but is not needed here. Sources: Kicks Digital Marketing 28 Sep 2026 (search partners on by default); IceBox Designs 2026; Google Ads Help 1722047; Practical Ecommerce. Confidence: High on networks and location, Med on ad rotation (no 2026 change found).

7. **PMax defaults to switch off if PMax is ever used: Final URL expansion, auto-generated video, text asset generation, AI voice-over.** Final URL expansion is on by default; turn off at creation or later at Campaign settings > Asset optimisation > Text customisation > Final URL > "Send traffic only to the URLs you've provided" (or add exclusions under "Exclude some URLs"). If an asset group has no video Google generates one from your images; the API documents an opt-out for "enhanced YouTube video generation" at the asset group level and the UI equivalent sits under asset group > Video enhancements. From 20 Mar 2026 Google added AI voice-overs to existing video assets unless advertisers opted out in video enhancement settings. Brand guidelines are now on automatically for new standard and retail PMax campaigns (exactly one business name, at least one logo; the migration deadline for old campaigns was 30 Oct 2025). Google rewrote the PMax Help Centre on 28 Aug 2026, so expect label changes. Sources: Google Ads Help 14337539; developers.google.com PMax assets and optimisations; ppc.land; blog.adnabu 2026. Confidence: High on Final URL expansion, Med on video and voice-over opt-out paths.

8. **Account creation flow (Oct 2026): Smart Mode is still the default, Expert Mode is a small link, and you can create an account with no campaign.** Flow: ads.google.com > Start now > sign in as endlesspks@gmail.com > business name / website screen > at the bottom of the goal screen click "Are you a professional marketer? Switch to Expert Mode" > on the campaign type screen click "Create an account without a campaign" (lower left) > confirm billing country Australia, time zone (Australia/Perth), currency AUD > Submit. Time zone and currency cannot be changed later. Billing is added afterwards under Billing > Settings. One guide claims the Smart to Expert switch is one-way; no source contradicts it. No source found describes a 2026 redesign of this flow, so treat labels as current but verify in UI. If the account instead opens on a campaign builder, look for "Switch to Expert Mode" under the wrench or Admin menu. Sources: Reliqus 2026; Clicks in Mind 2026; The PPC Machine; HawkSEM; Semrush. Confidence: Med (all secondary, 1 to 2 years old).

9. **Smart campaign and "guided" prompts: no forced upgrade found; the nudges are recommendations.** Nothing in 2026 sources describes Google forcing Smart campaigns to upgrade. The pressure comes through the Recommendations page ("Use Performance Max", "Add AI Max") and the goal-first campaign builder, where the escape hatch is "Create a campaign without a goal's guidance" at the goal step. For Endless Parks: never create a Smart campaign; if a dormant account contains one, pause it rather than accept any upgrade prompt. Sources: Stackmatix 2026; Hawky.ai 2026 roundup. Confidence: Med.

10. **Promo credit: an Australian spend A$600 get A$600 offer is still being reproduced in 2026, but only "new to Google Ads" advertisers qualify.** Conditions as reproduced: new to Google Ads, Australian billing address, one offer per advertiser, apply within 14 days of the first ad impression on your first Google Ads account, payment profile and valid payment method added at sign-up, accrue A$600 of costs excluding GST within 60 days (a payment of A$600 is not enough), credit applied within about 35 days, expires 60 days after it lands, covers future costs only. Google's own help page confirms the 60-day spend window and up to 35-day verification. Google Partners offers use a 20-days-from-creation rule instead. Tiers vary (a A$540 for A$540 tier was reported in Feb 2026). Where to enter: during sign-up, or Billing > Promotions > Manage promotional codes. Two cautions: a dormant endlesspks account that has ever served ads makes the business not "new", and at least one site (discovermybusiness.co) offers a "$600 match" in exchange for manager access, which is not a Google offer and should be ignored. At A$30/day the A$600 is reached in 20 days, well inside 60. Sources: Google Ads Help 2393021 (en-AU); Google Ads Help 15329625; alexandriatoday.com.au T&C reproduction; Overt Digital Feb 2026; Online Road 2026. Confidence: Med (no official ads.google.com AU offer page surfaced).

11. **Billing and GST: Google Australia Pty Ltd bills AU accounts and adds 10% GST; tax invoices come from Billing > Documents.** GST has applied since 1 Nov 2016. The monthly GST tax invoice (showing Google Australia's ABN and the GST amount) is at Billing > Documents > Tax and statutory documents, available by the 5th business day of the following month. Payment settings: automatic payments (default; card charged when a threshold is hit or every 30 days), manual payments (prepay, where offered), monthly invoicing (credit terms; historically requires 12 months of account history and about A$5,000 a month spend, so not relevant at A$900 a month). Put the trading entity and ABN in the payments profile exactly as on the ABR (Billing > Settings > Payments profile > Business name and address, account type Business, tax information). One agency guide claims a reverse-charge arrangement applies and that Google's documents are not valid tax invoices; this conflicts with the other sources and with Google's AU help page and should be disregarded unless Jake's accountant says otherwise. Sources: Google Ads Help 2375370 (en-AU); Google Ads Help 1723045 (en-AU); marketing.link.com.au; Click Click Media; Swoop Digital. Confidence: High on GST and document path, Med on monthly invoicing thresholds (older Google guidance).

12. **How to find out whether endlesspks@gmail.com already owns an Ads account.** Four checks, in order: (a) sign in at ads.google.com with that Google account; an existing account opens straight away with the 10-digit customer ID under the profile picture top right, several accounts show an account picker, and no account shows the "new account" sign-up flow (stop before submitting); (b) search the endlesspks Gmail for ads-noreply@google.com, "customer ID" and "Google Ads" billing emails; (c) payments.google.com lists every Google service attached to the payments profile; (d) Merchant Center 5382740922 > Settings (gear) > Access and services > Apps and services lists any linked Google Ads account with its customer ID and link status, and Shopify admin > Sales channels > Google & YouTube > Settings > Google services shows which Ads account the channel connected and which Google account did the connecting. Sources: Google Ads Help 1704344; Merchant Center Help 14528389; Shopify Help Center "connect". Confidence: High.

13. **Merchant Center linking, both directions, 2026 labels.** From Merchant Center (current UI): Settings (gear) > Access and services > Apps and services > Add service > Google Ads > enter the customer ID or pick an existing account > choose "link to Merchant Center only" rather than Business Manager unless Jake already uses Business Manager. From Google Ads: Admin > Linked accounts (some accounts show Tools > Data manager > Connected products) > Google Merchant Center > Link. If the same user is admin on both, the link approves automatically; otherwise approve in Merchant Center under Access and services or in Google Ads under Admin > Linked accounts. Both accounts must be free of account-level policy violations. Limits reported: 500 Ads accounts per Merchant Center. Since about 6 Jun 2026 Google has been automatically linking some Ads accounts to Merchant Center (targeted at accounts not running product ads) after an email with a "Connect now" option; no opt-out is documented, so check Admin > Linked accounts in any dormant account for a link Jake did not make. Sources: Merchant Center Help 12499498 and 14528389; developers.google.com Merchant Center linking; ppcnewsfeed May 2026; almcorp June 2026. Confidence: High on paths, Med on auto-linking details.

14. **Account-level negatives, shared lists and brand lists.** Account-level negative keywords: Admin > Account settings > Negative keywords > plus, one per line, 1,000 limit, applies to Search, Shopping and PMax search inventory. Shared negative keyword lists: Tools > Shared library > Exclusion lists > Negative keyword lists (apply to Standard Shopping and Search campaigns; PMax uses campaign-level negatives, added in Editor 2.10). Brand lists: Tools > Shared library > Brand lists; applied in PMax under campaign Settings > Other settings > Brand exclusions, and in Search under AI Max > Brand inclusions / exclusions. Account-level placement and IP exclusions arrived in Editor 2.11. For Endless Parks: put the R01 day-one negatives (free, printable, wallpaper, map pdf, jobs, camping ground, each state park name) in the account-level list so they also cover any future PMax. Sources: Google Ads Help 7102995, 2453983, 13607727; Optmyzr 2026; Editor release notes. Confidence: High.

15. **Scripts and automated rules worth running at A$30/day.** Path: Tools > Bulk actions > Scripts (scripts) and Tools > Bulk actions > Rules (automated rules). (a) Disapproved ads alert, Nils Rooijmans, free: needs only a Google Sheet URL and an email; emails campaign, ad group and policy reason. (b) Disapproved products alert, Nils Rooijmans, free: covers Shopping and PMax feed items with clicks. (c) Budget pacing: Cypress North's daily budget monitor, or Acuto's monthly pacer (alerts at 50% of monthly budget in the first 10 days, then 90% and 100%); Google's built-in Budget report (Campaigns > Budget report) works for Standard Shopping and Search but is not compatible with PMax. (d) Search term mining: an n-gram script (Brainlabs / Daniel Gilbert lineage) run monthly; Mike Rhodes' PMax Insights is paid and only relevant if PMax is added later. Automated rules to set on day one: pause any ad group with cost > A$40 and 0 conversions over 14 days (email only, do not auto-pause at launch), email when a campaign is Limited by budget, email on ad disapproval. Sources: ppc.io "37 scripts" 2026; Lunio 2026; Cypress North; Groas 2026; Adscriptly. Confidence: Med (script availability changes; test each in preview mode first).

16. **Dormant account clean-up order.** Google auto-archives conversion actions with no data for 13 months (Goals > Conversions > Summary > Remove inactive conversions, which archives and is reversible). An account with no activity is cancelled; only an Admin user can reactivate, and reactivation re-enables anything left Enabled, so pause everything first. After reactivation, three months with no serving cancels it again. Google may ask for payment verification at payments.google.com or identity documents (advertiser verification). Sources: Google Ads Help 10918269, 2375392, 7619129 (en-AU). Confidence: High.

17. **Users, MCC and change history.** Access levels: Admin, Standard, Read only, Email only, Billing. Add users at Admin > Access and security > Users > plus. Turn on "Require 2-step verification" at Admin > Access and security > Security. A manager account (MCC) is only worth creating if an agency or second business account arrives; it cannot set channel prioritisation and cannot itself be linked as a Merchant Center service in the way a client account can. Change history: Campaigns > Change history; filter User = "Auto-applied" or "Google Ads system" monthly to catch automation. Sources: Google Ads Help 9977851; Meticulosity; Bullseye 2026. Confidence: High on levels, Med on MCC linking nuance.

18. **Google Ads Editor 2.13 is current; 2.10 and earlier stop working on 19 Oct 2026.** 2.12 (Mar 2026) added 15 videos per PMax asset group and total budgets; 2.13 added classic Display assets for Demand Gen and the PMax customer retention goal. Use Editor for a full account export before any clean-up (Account > Export > Export whole account) and for bulk-building the Search keyword lists. Sources: Google Ads Editor Help 17136249, 16924635, 70133; ppcnewsfeed Mar 2026. Confidence: High.

19. **Shopify Google & YouTube channel specifics.** The Google account connected in Shopify needs Admin on the Ads account. In the channel's conversion tracking step, if the Ads account already has a purchase conversion, set the Shopify "Checkout completed" event to "Don't measure in linked account" to avoid double counting; enhanced conversions can be switched on in the channel settings after connection. Sources: Merchant Center Help 13494537 and 16267554; Google Ads Help 15496063 (en-AU). Confidence: High.

## 2. Setup checklist with menu paths

### A. Discovery (both cases, do first)
1. Sign in at ads.google.com as endlesspks@gmail.com. Record the customer ID (profile picture, top right) or note that the sign-up flow appeared and stop.
2. Merchant Center 5382740922 > Settings (gear) > Access and services > Apps and services: note any Google Ads customer ID and status.
3. Shopify admin > Sales channels > Google & YouTube > Settings > Google services: note the connected Google account and Ads account.
4. payments.google.com as endlesspks: note any Google Ads payments profile and card.

### B. New account
5. ads.google.com > Start now > "Are you a professional marketer? Switch to Expert Mode" > "Create an account without a campaign" > country Australia, time zone Australia/Perth, currency AUD > Submit.
6. Billing > Settings: payments profile type Business, legal name and ABN as on the ABR, address Balcatta WA 6021, automatic payments, add card. Billing > Promotions: enter any new advertiser code offered at sign-up within 14 days of the first impression.
7. Campaigns > Recommendations > Auto-apply > Manage: untick every box in both bundles > Save.
8. Campaigns > Assets > More > Account-level automated assets > More > Advanced settings: Automated promotions Off, Dynamic images Off, Longer ad headlines Off, Dynamic callouts Off, Dynamic structured snippets Off; leave Seller ratings and Dynamic sitelinks On.
9. Admin > Account settings: Negative keywords > add the R01 day-one list. Tracking > Final URL suffix: utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={campaignid}&utm_id={campaignid}&utm_content={creative}&utm_term={keyword}. Auto-tagging on. Check the Channel prioritisation card if it is present: Shopping, search partners Low 0% (or lowest allowed); Search, search partners Low 0% and Display Low 0%.
10. Admin > Access and security > Security: require 2-step verification. Users: Jake only at launch.
11. Admin > Linked accounts > Google Merchant Center > link 5382740922 (auto-approves if endlesspks is admin on both). Also link GA4 property (G-M3LTBNJ7H4) and Search Console.
12. Goals > Conversions: create or import the purchase conversion before connecting Shopify; then in Shopify's channel set "Checkout completed" to "Don't measure in linked account" if a purchase action already exists, and turn on enhanced conversions.
13. Tools > Shared library > Brand lists: create "Endless Parks" (endless parks, endlesspks, endless parks bottle) for later PMax exclusion and AI Max inclusion.
14. Tools > Bulk actions > Scripts: install the disapproved ads alert, disapproved products alert and a monthly pacing script; preview, then schedule daily. Tools > Bulk actions > Rules: the three email rules from finding 15.
15. Per campaign at creation: AI Max Off; networks search partners and Display unticked; Locations Australia, Location options "Presence"; languages English; no ad schedule; budget per R01. Name campaigns ep_[type]_[product]_au_[period], for example ep_shop_bottle_au_2026q4, ep_srch_brand_au_2026q4, ep_srch_nonbrand_au_2026q4, mirroring the Meta convention; add labels Launch-Oct26 and BFCM where relevant. No shared budgets at this spend.
16. Download Google Ads Editor 2.13; Account > Export whole account as the day-one backup.

### C. Dormant account found
17. Before touching anything: Editor > Export whole account; Campaigns > Change history > export last 2 years; Billing > Documents > download past invoices; note past spend and any old PMax created by the Shopify channel.
18. Pause every campaign (Campaigns > select all > Edit > Pause) before reactivating or adding billing, because reactivation re-enables anything left Enabled.
19. Billing > Settings: update payments profile and card; complete any verification request at payments.google.com; add ABN.
20. Steps 7 to 10 and 13 to 14 above (auto-apply, automated assets, negatives, URL suffix, security, brand list, scripts).
21. Admin > Linked accounts: confirm Merchant Center 5382740922 is the only Merchant Center link (remove any auto-link to a different Merchant Center); confirm GA4 and Search Console.
22. Goals > Conversions > Summary: Remove inactive conversions (13 months); set remaining old actions to Secondary; keep one Primary purchase action with value; check Goals > Conversions > Settings for account default goals and attribution model.
23. Tools > Shared library: review old negative keyword lists and audiences; Audience manager > delete stale lists only if unused.
24. Campaigns: remove (not just pause) old Smart campaigns and any Shopify-created PMax; create the R01 campaigns fresh rather than editing old ones, so learning and settings start clean.
25. Campaigns > Change history: filter by "Auto-applied" for 2025 to 2026 to see what Google changed while the account was idle, and reverse anything still live.

## 3. Where sources disagree

- Automated promotions eligibility: ppc.land's first report limited it to campaigns with location assets and no manual promotion assets; later reporting of Google's email describes broad automatic opt-in. My read: assume opted in, switch it off.
- Channel prioritisation 0%: Search Engine Land and ppc.land say 0% is selectable; the Help page extract only confirms High/Low plus a percentage cap. My read: try 0%, fall back to the lowest allowed, and keep the legacy network checkboxes unticked too, since both systems coexist until February 2027.
- GST treatment: one agency guide claims a reverse charge with no GST on the invoice; Google's AU help page and every other source say Google Australia Pty Ltd charges 10% GST and issues a tax invoice. My read: GST is charged and claimable on the BAS if Endless Parks is GST registered.
- Promo credit timing: 14 days from first impression (standard new advertiser offer) versus 20 days from account creation (Google Partners offers). Both are correct for different offer types.
- Expert Mode switch being permanent: one secondary source only. Irrelevant in practice, since Expert Mode is what Jake wants.
- Merchant Center linking from an MCC: one guide says an Ads manager cannot be linked; Google documentation allows Merchant Center to link to manager accounts. Not needed here either way.
- Monthly invoicing eligibility: described loosely as "history of on-time payments" in AU guides; older Google guidance names 12 months and about A$5,000 a month. Either way, not available at A$900 a month.
