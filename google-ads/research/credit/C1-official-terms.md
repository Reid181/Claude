# C1: Google Ads new advertiser promotional credit, Australia, as at 15 Oct 2026

Prepared for Endless Parks (Perth, AUD, GST registered, A$30/day). Method: 22 extended web searches plus one blocked fetch of ads.google.com/intl/en_au (ENOTFOUND). No Google AU offer landing page or AU promotional terms page surfaced, so the current A$ figure rests on secondary sources. Google Help wording is quoted from search extracts of the live pages, accessed 15 Oct 2026.

## 1. Findings

1. **Eligibility (Google's words): account under 14 days old at redemption, valid payment method, no prior Google Ads for the same business.** Help 6388096: "Your account must be less than 14 days old at the time of redemption", "you must have a valid payment method added", and you "cannot have advertised with Google Ads before using a different account for the same business". Also: "an eligibility check will be triggered for up to 35 days once you meet the offer's requirements" and "criteria for promotions varies, so review your offer for details on the spend requirement and the potential credit amount". https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6388096. Confidence High. The clock is account age, not first impression (section 3).

2. **Spend window 60 days, verification up to 35 days, credit life 60 days.** Help 2393021 (en-AU): "New advertisers promotions typically require spend to be completed within 60 days to be eligible for the credit. After completing the required spend, there is an up to 35-day verification period that confirms an advertiser is new to Google." A sister page: "advertisers normally have 60 days to spend their earned promotional credit after it has been added to their account". https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2393021?hl=en-AU. Confidence High.

3. **Policy: apply at sign-up or within 14 days of creating the account; expired offers are dead; advertising only.** Policies Help 1396257: "most offers need to be applied at sign up, others need to be applied within 14 days after you first create your Google Ads account"; "Google cannot accept or replace expired promotional offers"; "Google can't provide promotional offers retroactively"; "Promotional credits can only be used towards advertising. If you cancel your account before spending the entire credit, the remaining balance can't be refunded." https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/1396257 (cache about 4.5 years old, still live). Confidence High on wording, Med on age.

4. **Only post-redemption spend counts; credit covers future costs only.** Help 16915411: for postpay (automatic payments) the credit covers "future advertising costs", costs before it is applied or after it runs out go to the payment method; "Only spend after redeeming the offer counts toward it"; "Once active (after spent requirements of your promotion are met), a promotion credit will be used up in full before we use your existing balance or charge your instrument on file"; "Any unspent credit will roll over to the next billing cycle". For prepay, redeem by the offer's expiry and pay in "somewhat above the requirement" because serving may stop early. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16915411. Confidence High. Automatic payments are not a stated condition; AU accounts are normally postpay anyway.

5. **How it appears in Billing.** Same page: "The portion of the promotion spent will appear as a sub-line under the 'Active promotions' section in the current month's card on the 'Billing Summary' page within about 35 days." Help 16323427 gives the statuses, including "Processing: The spend requirement has been met. Our system is currently validating the account's eligibility", plus a "Spent from Promotion" column. Billing activity (11349322): an active promotion "will show as a unique item (not a daily item)". Billing Summary (11348111): "Net cost" is "the cost of campaigns and taxes or fees, if applicable ... minus any adjustments and credits". Confidence High.

6. **Where to enter a code.** Help 7646208: "select the Billing icon, then choose Promotions. From there, select the blue plus button, enter the offer, and submit it. The credit will be applied after your account is eligible per the offer's terms and conditions." Help 16324294: manager accounts "can't enter a promotional offer from the manager level"; codeless offers show on the same Promotions page with an apply button. Google's business page: "new advertisers will receive an ad credit after meeting the minimum spend requirement for the selected offer" (accessed 15 Oct 2026). Confidence High.

7. **The current AU amounts: two offer families, both secondary.** (a) Flat A$600 for A$600: Hostinger's partner terms say the Australian offer "requires AUD 600 of spend within 60 days to unlock AUD 600 in credit" (undated); Online Road (2026): "spend a set amount (often $600) within your first 60 days"; Ranking Co (undated): "New clients must spend above $600 AUD over the first 60 days"; webnots (late Jul 2026) lists Australia as "$600 to $1,200". (b) Tiered A$540 ladder: All Marketing, Perth: "New Google Ads Accounts Only Offer Ends 30/9/26", "$540 in ad credit when you spend $540", "spend $2,900 → get $1,500", "spend $9,000 → get $4,500", "or as amended by Google's promotional terms" (cache mid Sep 2026). Overt Digital, Feb 2026, lists the full ladder (spend/credit): 540/540, 720/650, 1,450/1,000, 2,900/1,500, 4,000/2,000, 7,500/3,750, 9,000/4,500, and says "Not every advertiser sees every tier ... offers are often attached to particular signup paths, partner referrals or invitations." Confidence Med that both families ran in 2026, Low on which one Jake sees on 15 Oct 2026 given the 30 Sep end date. The 720, 1,450, 4,000 and 7,500 tiers are single source (Overt).

8. **Partner offers (only via an agency): 20 days from account creation.** Help 15329625 (en-AU): "Promotional credits are available to Premier Partners and Partners for eligible accounts within 20 days of advertiser's account creation"; example "spend $500 USD (or local equivalent) in your first 60 days* and get $500 USD in ad credit", the 60 days "counted from offer redemption"; partners "choose from 7 credit values to manually apply to specific clients" (cache about May 2026). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15329625?hl=en-AU. Confidence High. Seven values match the seven Overt tiers, so the A$540 ladder is probably the Partner ladder in AUD.

9. **2026 changes: nothing found that removed Australia; new US-first offers appeared.** ppcnewsfeed 21 May 2026: "limited-time 2× incentive for new advertisers", $500 spend earns $1,000, $680 earns $1,360, $1,400 earns $2,800; almcorp: "initial rollout targeted U.S. advertisers", "caps are published inside the sign-up flow based on country and currency". ppcnewsfeed Aug 2026: "up to $1,500 in ad credit when they create their first Demand Gen campaign", by promo code (Adweek confirms). ppcnewsfeed Jul 2026 describes a "$20,000 Ad Credit Match" for larger advertisers. Google Ads API: "As of v23, the API supports only ACQUISITION type incentives, which is for new accounts only". No source says Australia lost the offer. Confidence Med; AU availability of the 2x offer rests on no source.

10. **GST and the threshold.** Google's pages are silent. discovermybusiness.co (undated, flagged in R10 for asking for manager access) says "GST and administrative fees don't count toward the AUD 600 goal" and suggests "about AUD 660 including GST"; R10's T&C reproduction (alexandriatoday) also said ex GST. Google Ads reports campaign cost ex GST and adds 10% on the invoice (Help 2375370 en-AU), so "Spent from Promotion" will track ex GST cost. Confidence Med (two secondary sources).

11. **Campaign types.** No Google page restricts the general new advertiser credit by campaign type; policy says only "Promotional credits can only be used towards advertising". Comradeweb (third party) says the credit "isn't locked to one campaign type". Only the Demand Gen offer (finding 9) is campaign specific. Shopping and Search spend should count and be covered. Confidence Med.

12. **Shopify's own Google credit appears gone.** Shopify's "$500 USD in ad credit when the merchant spent $500 USD within 60 days" page is about four years old; dodropshipping (2026): "as of 2026, that broad Shopify-linked Google Ads credit is no longer the main route". Old Shopify Community threads on that promo reported "one code per advertiser" and "only spend accrued after code application counts". Confidence Low (single 2026 source).

13. **Enforcement risk is real.** A Blind post describes a user who spent over $500 within 60 days and was told they had breached the terms with no reason given. Single unverified source, consistent with the 35-day "new to Google" check. Confidence Low.

- Keep automatic payments with a card. The card pays the qualifying spend plus 10% GST; the credit then covers future ad cost before the card is charged again.
- Tier choice at A$30/day: 60 days of full delivery is A$1,800 ex GST, but a new Shopping campaign on a fresh feed often underdelivers. Flat A$600 offer: threshold about day 20 (A$660 inc GST on the card). Missing a tier means no credit, so pick the tier below expected spend.
- Flat offer timeline: redeem 16 Oct, threshold about 5 Nov, credit lands by about 10 Dec at the latest, expires about 8 Feb 2027. A$600 is 20 days of spend, funding part of the Christmas run or January.
- Assume the threshold is ex GST and budget A$660 of card charges. Ask the accountant how GST shows once the credit is consumed; no source states the GST treatment of a promotional credit.
- Ignore the Demand Gen and 2x offers and any site offering a "$600 match" for account access. One offer per advertiser is the rule in every T&C reproduction found.

## 3. Where sources disagree

- Deadline to apply: Google Help 6388096 and policy 1396257 say 14 days from account creation; older AU T&C reproductions, the plati.market listing, Justia and Shopify Community say 14 days from first ad impression. Apply at sign-up and both are satisfied.
- Amount: flat A$600 (Hostinger, Online Road, Ranking Co, webnots) versus the A$540 ladder (All Marketing, Overt). Probably both live through different channels: A$600 on public sign-up, the ladder via Partners. The 30 Sep 2026 end date suggests quarterly rotation, so expect a refreshed Q4 offer.
- Start of the 60 days: "from offer redemption" (Partners page, 16915411, webnots) versus "first 60 days" of the account (agency copy). Google's wording wins: redemption.
- Credit landing: "about five business days" (webnots, bangmedia) versus "up to 35 days" (Google). Plan on 35.
- GST exclusion: two secondary sources, no Google confirmation. 2x offer in Australia: no source either way. Blend AI (2024) claims a 30-day spend window; Google says 60.
