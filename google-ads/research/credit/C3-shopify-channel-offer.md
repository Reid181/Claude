# C3: Google Ads credit via the Shopify Google & YouTube channel, and account creation inside Shopify (Oct 2026)

Prepared 15 Oct 2026 for Endless Parks (Perth, AUD, channel connected as endlesspks@gmail.com, Merchant Center 5382740922). Builds on R10 finding 10 and R10 section 2.

Method: 27 extended web searches on 15 Oct 2026; a direct fetch of shopify.com/au/google was refused (ENOTFOUND), so every quote is from a search extract of the named page.

## 1. Findings

1. **Shopify still advertises a USD "spend $500 get $500" credit on its global and Australian Google & YouTube pages, on a page about four years old.** shopify.com/google and shopify.com/au/google: "To help you get started with Google Ads, enjoy $500 USD in ad credit when you spend $500 USD within 60 days. Terms and Conditions apply." Search metadata dates the global page at about 1,435 days old (late 2022). No AUD figure appears on Shopify's site. Sources: shopify.com/google, shopify.com/au/google (accessed 15 Oct 2026). Confidence: High that the wording is live, Low that it is a separate offer an AU account can claim today.

2. **The terms behind Shopify's "$500" are Google's standard new advertiser terms, not a Shopify programme.** Shopify's hosted Google promo guide (cdn.shopify.com, 2022) reproduces them: for "advertisers new to Google Ads, with a billing address in the United States"; the code "has to be applied to your first Google Ads account within 14 days of your first ad impression"; "advertising campaigns must accrue costs of at least $500, (excluding any taxes or applicable fees), within 60 days. Making a payment of $500 is not sufficient"; "The credit then expires 60 days after it is applied to your account"; full terms at google.com/ads/coupons/terms. Apart from amount and country these match the AU reproduction in R10 finding 10. Source: cdn.shopify.com 2022 Peak Season Guide PDF. Confidence: High that it was the standard offer, Med that nothing newer replaced it.

3. **The Shopify channel cannot create a Google Ads account, and no source shows it applying a promo code.** Shopify Help Center, connect page: "To set up Google Ads, you must have an existing Google Ads account." A "Create new" option exists only for Merchant Center. Merchant Center Help 13421604: "you'll need to create and/or link a Google Ads account and set up billing in Merchant Center", via "Scroll down to the Performance Max campaign module. Select your Google Ads account and click Connect. Click Create Campaign. You'll be redirected to the Ads onboarding flow in Merchant Center." Google's sign-up page says the offer "will automatically be applied to your new Google Ads account upon sign-up" and "You will see the offer when you enter your billing information". No source describes the offer appearing inside Merchant Center onboarding. Sources: help.shopify.com connect page; Merchant Center Help 13421604; business.google.com/aunz/google-ads (accessed 15 Oct 2026). Confidence: High on Shopify's wording, Med on the Merchant Center path, Low on whether that flow surfaces the offer.

4. **Shopify merchants saw a targeted "$500 Google Ads credit" notification in admin in September 2025; replies warn that prior accounts disqualify you.** Thread 566190 (Sep 2025) is merchants asking whether the notification is real. Thread 127451: "check Billing > Summary > Promotions to verify credits"; "if you have ever had a previous Google Ads account, promotions are not availalbe" (sic). Neither thread gives an AU amount or an automatic code. Sources: community.shopify.com threads 566190 and 127451. Confidence: Med (forum).

5. **One 2026 guide says the Shopify route has faded.** dodropshipping.com: "Google previously ran a free-credit promo through Shopify, but as of 2026, that broad Shopify-linked Google Ads credit is no longer the main route". Single secondary source. Confidence: Low.

6. **The standard offer in Oct 2026 is tiered, chosen at the billing step, and Google's AU/NZ page shows a "2X" variant with placeholder amounts.** business.google.com/aunz/google-ads: "spend a set amount with Google Ads in the first 60 days to unlock the credit"; "meet the spend requirement of your offer and get 2X back in ad credit"; "Choose an offer to earn up to {$ion-cyo-award-amount--max} in ad credit". ppcnewsfeed (May 2026), USD examples: "Spending $500 earns $1,000 in ads credit, while $680 yields $1,360, and a $1,400 spend results in $2,800 of credit", with no end date. AU third parties disagree: Overt Digital (Feb 2026) A$540 for A$540 up to A$4,500 for A$9,000; Bang Media "up to $1,800"; allmarketing.com.au "up to $4,500", ending "30/9/26 or as amended by Google's promotional terms"; Online Road "$600". Confidence: High that the offer is tiered and selected when billing is entered, Low on AU amounts (all secondary).

7. **Google's eligibility wording, Oct 2026.** Help 16324294: "You're considered a new advertiser if you're creating an account for a business that has never advertised with Google Ads, or if you're expanding an existing business into a new country with a new billing address"; new advertiser promotions are claimed "during sign-up"; "you'll first need a payment method on file"; and "the offer will be invalidated and the amount lost when you switch to invoicing". Advertising Policies 1396257: "most offers need to be applied at sign up, while others need to be applied within 14 days after you first create your Google Ads account"; "Google cannot accept or replace expired promotional offers". Help 6388096 (per extract) adds "less than 14 days old at the time of redemption" and "cannot have advertised with Google Ads before using a different account for the same business". Help 2393021 (en-AU): "New advertisers promotions typically require spend to be completed within 60 days to be eligible for the credit. After completing the required spend, there is an up to 35-day verification period before the credit is released." "Upon the promotional credit being added to the advertiser's account, the advertiser typically has 60 days to spend their earned credit." Help 16323427 lists statuses Redeemed, Processing ("may take up to 35 days"), Active, Expired and Invalidated. Sources: support.google.com answers 16324294, 1396257, 6388096, 2393021 (en-AU), 16323427 (accessed 15 Oct 2026). Confidence: High.

8. **Offers do not stack: one promotion per account.** Adcore's Couponer (Google Premier Partner codes) requires that "the account has no other promotions applied", "the advertiser is new to Google Ads", caps codes at "up to $500 (USD or currency equivalent)", and says "each account is eligible for one coupon only". Help 15329625 (en-AU) treats Partner offers as a separate programme. No source describes a Shopify credit stacking on the standard offer. Sources: adcore.com; Help 15329625 (en-AU). Confidence: Med.

9. **Connecting the Ads account to the channel does not create a campaign; a PMax campaign exists only if someone clicks "Create Campaign", which redirects into Merchant Center and demands a daily budget.** Shopify Help Center: "You can create new Performance Max campaigns in Google Merchant Center, which can be accessed from the Google & YouTube channel in your Shopify admin." Google Ads Help 10431635 says the same: campaigns are created in Merchant Center after billing is set up in Google Ads. Merchant Center Help 13421604: you "set a daily budget" and a name, then Create; on disconnect "your active Performance Max campaigns will continue to run without using your product data". Confidence: High.

10. **Linking does create conversion actions, with purchase as account default.** Merchant Center Help 13421604: "A purchase conversion action is created automatically in Google Ads and set as the account default"; add to cart and begin checkout are "set as secondary conversion actions, which are included for observation only and don't affect bidding". Google Ads Help 13494537 (about 1,165 days old) calls them "the suggested default" and offers "choose not to measure in the linked account". Confidence: High that they exist after linking, Med on automatic versus pre-ticked.

11. **Smart Mode risk in guided flows.** Guides say new accounts "start in Smart Mode by default"; FeedArmy documents a bug where a switched account shows "the Smart campaign setup screen again" on next login. The Merchant Center flow is campaign first and cannot yield an empty Expert Mode account. Sources: feedarmy.com; megadigital.ai; sixthcitymarketing.com. Confidence: Med.

12. **Other 2026 Google credits are existing account promotions: a PMax credit "expired July 18" 2026 and a Demand Gen "up to $1,500" offer (12 Aug 2026) needing a code and a first Demand Gen campaign.** Neither stacks (finding 8) and R01 rules both out. Source: ppcnewsfeed Jul and Aug 2026. Confidence: Med.

## 2. What this means for Endless Parks

- **No separate Shopify credit exists to chase.** The "$500 USD" on shopify.com/au/google is a four year old reproduction of Google's standard offer. The only credit is the one shown at the ads.google.com billing step, in AUD, at that day's tier. Treat any Shopify admin banner as a pointer to the same offer.
- **Create the account at ads.google.com, not from Merchant Center or the channel.** Shopify cannot create one (finding 3); the Merchant Center route is a PMax builder that spends from day one (finding 9); nothing shows the offer surfacing there (findings 3 and 6); and the guided flow risks Smart Mode (finding 11). Follow R10 section 2 step 5: Expert Mode, "Create an account without a campaign", Australia, Australia/Perth, AUD.
- **Sequence to the 14 day and 60 day clocks.** Redemption happens when billing is entered and must fall within 14 days of account creation; the 60 day spend window starts at redemption and earlier spend does not count (finding 7). So finish what needs no Ads account first (feed fixes, Merchant Center approvals), then create the account, add the card and accept the offer in one sitting, and launch Shopping within days. At A$30/day ex GST (the threshold is "excluding any taxes") A$600 is reached in 20 days while A$1,800 needs all 60 days with no slack: pick a tier whose spend requirement is at most about A$900, preferring any 2X tier at or below that. Any credit up to A$1,800 is spendable inside its own 60 day life.
- **Eligibility hinges on "a business that has never advertised with Google Ads".** If endlesspks@gmail.com ever owned an account that served an ad for Endless Parks, the new account can show "Invalidated" after the 35 day check. Run the R10 finding 12 checks first and screenshot the offer terms shown at sign-up.
- **Payment setting:** automatic payments on a business card, payments profile per R10. Never request monthly invoicing on this account.
- **Connect the channel after the account exists:** Shopify admin, Sales channels, Google & YouTube, Google Ads, Get started, choose the new customer ID (endlesspks must be admin). Accept purchase as account default, leave add to cart and begin checkout secondary, turn on enhanced conversions. Never click "Create Campaign" in the Performance Max module; check Campaigns in Google Ads the next day for anything created outside Google Ads.
- **After redemption:** Billing, Promotions should move from "Redeemed" to "Processing" to "Active"; chase support if nothing moves 35 days after the threshold.

## 3. Where sources disagree

- **Amount and currency:** Shopify says $500 USD; AU agencies say A$540, A$600, up to A$1,800 or up to A$4,500; Google's AU/NZ page shows placeholders and "2X". My read: budget on A$600 matched; the tier shown at billing is the only truth.
- **Start of the 14 days:** Google's policy page and Help 6388096 count from account creation; the 2022 Shopify hosted terms and the AU reproduction count from first ad impression. My read: satisfy both by entering billing on creation day.
- **Whether Shopify still has a credit:** Shopify's page says yes; dodropshipping (single source) says it has faded. My read: it was never separate, so moot.
- **Conversion actions:** Merchant Center Help says created automatically on link; Google Ads Help says "suggested default". My read: audit Goals, Conversions after linking.
- **Offer end date:** allmarketing.com.au shows 30 Sep 2026; Google's page has none. My read: evergreen with changing tiers; the date was that agency's own campaign.
