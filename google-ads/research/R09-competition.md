# R09: Competitive landscape on Google AU for the Parks Bottle and the Aussie Made tees

Prepared 15 Oct 2026 for Endless Parks. Scope: who competes on Google Search and Shopping in Australia for the launch products (A$87 Australian National and Marine Parks Bottle, 1.2 L, straw lid, sticker pack included; four A$69 Australian Made tees), what they say, what they charge, and what the queries are likely to cost.

## 0. Method and a hard limitation to read first

- 26 web searches (extended mode) were run on 15 Oct 2026. Every direct page fetch failed: the session's network policy returned 403 on the proxy CONNECT for all 31 hosts probed, including adstransparency.google.com, google.com.au, shopping.google.com, trends.google.com, bing.com, duckduckgo.com, letswander.co, awesome-maps.com, endlessparks.com, frankgreen.com.au, kathmandu.com.au, anaconda.com.au, snowys.com.au, wildearth.com.au, bcf.com.au, macpac.com.au, au.stanley1913.com, au.yeti.com, hydroflask.com.au, semrush.com, ahrefs.com, neilpatel.com, keywordseverywhere.com, wordstream.com, etsy.com, amazon.com.au, spyfu.com, similarweb.com and web.archive.org.
- Consequence: no live AU SERP, no Google Shopping AU grid, no Google Ads Transparency Center lookups and no Google Trends AU could be observed. Everything below on advertisers is either (a) what the search index shows about who sells and who publishes gift pages for these queries, or (b) inference from category knowledge, and each is labelled. The search tool itself is US-centric, so AU ad presence cannot be seen through it either.
- Fix: in the cloud environment menu in the session title bar choose Edit, then under Network access either pick a broader level or add the hosts above under Allowed domains (keep Allow package managers ticked). The Transparency Center and Google AU lookups in section 4 can then be rerun in about 20 minutes, or Jake can run them in Chrome using the exact list in section 4.
- Keyword volume and CPC: only three real Australian numbers were found (all for broad apparel terms). For every niche query the table gives a practitioner estimate range, labelled "EP est." with Low reliability, to be replaced by Google Keyword Planner data the day the Google Ads account exists.

## 1. Findings

1. **The "national parks water bottle" category on Google is a US category, and Endless Parks is the only Australian product in it.** The index for "national parks water bottle" returns Etsy listings (32 oz insulated stainless with US park stickers), Amazon US (BlueTrail B0CLT6599Q, the Erika Chesonis collab B0CDVK9WKJ, WANDERSTATE B0DHD3VMP3 with 63 stickers and two lids), Underdog Games "Trekking the National Parks" bottle (63 stickers, 3 lid options), Let's Wander, GEOPROJECT and Rocky Mountain Conservancy. The three endlessparks.com pages (/pages/adventurebottle, /products/ultimate-adventure-bottle, the collection) rank alongside them. Source: search results 15 Oct 2026 (etsy.com/listing/4301200689, amazon.com/dp/B0CLT6599Q, underdoggames.com, endlessparks.com). Confidence High that the organic set is US-dominated; Medium that the same holds for the AU paid SERP (unobserved).

2. **Let's Wander is cheap, US-only and sale-driven.** letswander.co lists the 32 oz (946 ml) National Parks Water Bottle with 63 US park stickers at "Regular price $99.00, Sale price $44.95, 50% off" (USD, about A$68); Amazon US lists the Original Black at US$39.95 with 4.6 stars from 373 ratings; the site claims "Rated 4.7/5 from 2000+ happy customers". Reviews recur on sticker peeling and one broken handle. The product is useless to an Australian buyer (US parks only) but its ad copy pattern (always-on 50% off, "USA hiker gift bottle", bucket list) is what Endless Parks will be compared to if Let's Wander geo-targets AU. Source: letswander.co/products/national-parks-water-bottle, amazon.com/dp/B0D8FP9SLZ, 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High on prices as indexed; Low on whether it advertises in AU.

3. **Awesome Maps is the one sticker-bottle competitor that explicitly ships from an Australian warehouse.** The Adventure Bottle World (1 L, 47 stickers, world map not Australia) is listed at US$69.95 crossed out from US$99.90 (about A$105 before freight), "ships from warehouses in Germany, the US and Australia", AU delivery US$7.90. No Australia-specific design exists. Woodstock Shop resells it at EUR 55. Source: awesome-maps.com/products/adventure-bottle-world, woodstockshop.com, 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High on listing; Medium on AU ad activity (an AU warehouse usually means AU targeting).

4. **Generic 1.2 L / 40 oz straw bottles in Australia sell for A$29 to A$80, so "1.2l water bottle australia" is a price-led query Endless Parks cannot win on price.** Power Drink Hydration 1.2 L A$42 (on sale A$29.40, personalisation offered); Amazon AU: BUZIO A$29.74 to A$39.99, SANTECO A$29.59 to A$36.99, OZERO A$29.99 to A$35.99, Ello Cooper 40 oz A$42.03, Simple Modern 40 oz A$53.70, Takeya 40 oz A$57.48; Matchbox lists Owala FreeSip 40 oz at A$79.95. Source: powerdrinkhydration.com.au, amazon.com.au search pages, matchbox.com.au, 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High on prices as indexed (snapshots, may be stale).

5. **The brand name "endless parks" is clean in the index: every result is Endless Parks owned or about it, plus one reseller.** Results: endlessparks.com pages, Pinterest, TikTok @endlessparks, Facebook, Instagram, ZoeWanderingWaves blog, and tinytreasuresdenmark.com (a Denmark WA gift shop) selling the "Endless Parks Water Bottle" at A$85.00, in stock. No evidence of another brand targeting the name. Source: search 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High for organic; Low for paid (ads unobserved). Note: the reseller at A$85 is A$2 under the A$87 PDP; if Tiny Treasures ever lists in Merchant Center it will show cheaper in the Shopping grid for brand queries.

6. **Google's index holds four different prices for the bottle (A$58, A$63 sold out, A$85, A$87).** The /collections/all snapshot shows $58.00, the homepage snapshot $63.00 marked sold out, the reseller $85, the PDP $87. Stale pages are harmless organically but are a Merchant Center risk: price mismatch between feed and landing page is a disapproval trigger, and a "sold out" marketing page (/pages/adventurebottle) is a poor ad landing page. Source: search snippets 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High that the inconsistency exists in the index; Medium on current live state (site not fetchable).

7. **Gift queries are owned by big-box outdoor retailers and marketplaces, not by drinkware brands.** "camping gifts australia": BCF ("BCFing Christmas" 2026 gift guide and /christmas/camping-gift-ideas), Anaconda /d/gifts-for-camping, Snowys gift ideas (free delivery over A$69, Zip and Afterpay), Amazon AU, Man of Many. "gifts for hikers australia": Paddy Pallin (dedicated gift pages, free shipping over A$99), Macpac /gifts-for-hikers, Biome, We Are Explorers, Etsy AU. "gifts for campers": Amazon AU, Adventure Awaits, Etsy AU, Caravan Mods, Dad Shop (A$15 to A$40), AMD Touring (an "Australian Made Camping Gifts" collection), Campera. These retailers run always-on Shopping and Search and will push gift CPCs up from mid November. Source: bcf.com.au/christmas, anacondastores.com, snowys.com.au, paddypallin.com.au, macpac.com.au, 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High that these pages exist; Medium that they are advertised (inferred from scale).

8. **"gifts for grey nomads" is a thin, low-competition query with no big-box owner.** Results: Redbubble and CafePress novelty merch (CafePress tee A$19.49), Etsy AU, Adventure Awaits (caravan gifts, ships daily from VIC), Rescue Swag blog, Grey Nomads Australia forum, Van Go Collections (A$72 dinnerware), thegreynomads.com.au. No Kathmandu, BCF or Anaconda page targets the phrase. Source: search 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High on organic; Medium that paid competition is light.

9. **"travel gifts australia" is split between souvenir shops and outdoor retailers, with "Australian made" as an existing selling angle.** Bits of Australia (Australian made travel gifts, AUD), Paddy Pallin travel gifts (includes water bottles, free shipping over A$99), Adventure Awaits (road trip value pack A$49.95 on sale), Gifts Australia, Spoilt, Australia The Gift (USD), Etsy. Source: search 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High.

10. **Australian made tees: the query space is crowded by cheap print-on-demand, while genuinely Australian made tees sit at A$50 to A$160.** Australian Made Clothes A$24 to A$29; Threadheads A$32 sale, buy 3 get 20% off (Australian themed, origin not stated); Redbubble from A$19.54; Etsy low A$20s; Everything Australian lists a D-Stall Works heavyweight box tee marked Australian Made at A$50; Vege Threads (Melbourne made, organic) A$60 to A$70 per an older listing; Bassike (Ethical Clothing Australia certified) A$160 in a 2026 Broadsheet piece; Citizen Wolf (Sydney, made to order), Kuwaii (Brunswick), The Organic T-shirt (NSW) have no indexed prices. Endless Parks at A$69 sits with Vege Threads, above the novelty tier and below designer basics. Source: australianmadeclothes.com.au, threadheads.com, everythingaustralian.com.au, panaprium.com, broadsheet.com.au, thegreenhubonline.com, 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High on prices as indexed.

11. **The only real Australian keyword volumes found are broad apparel terms.** Semrush public domain page for threadheads.com.au (Australia): "t shirts" 26,600 per month, "graphic tees clothing" 13,600. Ahrefs public page for factorie.com.au: "graphic tees" 8.1K per month in AU. A HI Agency fashion SEO guide states "australian made clothing" is a long-tail term with low volume and higher conversion, without a number. Source: cw.semrush.com/website/threadheads.com.au/overview (updated 13 Dec 2025), ahrefs.com/websites/factorie.com.au, hiagency.au. Confidence Medium (tool estimates, 10 months old).

12. **Australian CPC anchors.** Rocking Web (Perth agency, 2025 benchmarks): Australia all-industry average CPC about A$4.12 (US$2.69); e-commerce A$1.82; Clothing and Apparel Shopping CPC US$0.69 (global). FoundryCRO 2026 Shopping benchmarks: Apparel/Fashion average CPC US$0.89, CTR 1.0 to 1.2%, CVR 2.4%, CPA US$37.08, ROAS 6.1x. Triple Whale and others put apparel Shopping CPC at US$0.42 to US$0.89. Anahaw Media's 2026 apparel CPC of US$4.44 is US Search data and says so. No source breaks out drinkware. Source: rockingweb.com.au/google-ads-benchmarks-by-industry-2025, foundrycro.com, triplewhale.com/blog/google-ads-benchmarks, anahawmedia.com.au. Confidence Medium for the AU e-commerce A$1.82 figure; Low for applying apparel Shopping CPCs to drinkware.

13. **Frank Green is an organic and social giant, not visibly a Search advertiser.** marketinglab.com.au: about 581,262 monthly visits, 37.4K ranking keywords in AU, and the article recommends the brand "mirror its SEO list in Google paid campaigns", implying light paid Search at the time. TikTok for Business has a frank green case study. Canstar (Feb 2026) rated Yeti top (range A$50 to A$100) and Frank Green three stars; a Sept 2026 US piece reports Frank Green going viral on TikTok and US search interest surging. Source: marketinglab.com.au, ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/frank-green, au.lifestyle.yahoo.com (Canstar), wixx.com 10 Sep 2026. Confidence Medium. Stanley, Yeti, Hydro Flask, Kathmandu, Macpac, Anaconda, BCF, Snowys and Wild Earth Transparency Center records could not be retrieved (blocked); treat all as active Shopping advertisers on generic bottle and gift terms by default.

14. **"sticker bottle" and "adventure bottle" carry name collisions.** "sticker bottle" indexes to Etsy US sticker packs and Nalgene sticker bottles and GEOPROJECT (sticker pack US$14.95, bottles US$32 to US$52): intent is mixed between buying a bottle and buying stickers. "adventure bottle" indexes to Awesome Maps "Adventure Bottle World" and "Dive Adventure Bottle", Flashbay promotional bottles, and Endless Parks' own "Ultimate Adventure Bottle" pages. Endless Parks ranks for its own naming but shares the phrase with Awesome Maps. Source: search 15 Oct 2026. Confidence High.

## 3. What this means for Endless Parks

**Bid on (non-brand Search, exact and phrase, Manual CPC capped about A$1.00 to A$1.30 as R01 recommends):**
- "national parks water bottle", "national park water bottle", "national park bottle australia", "australian national parks bottle", "parks water bottle", "national and marine parks bottle". Nobody Australian competes; the US sellers' copy is US-park specific and their landing pages charge US dollars plus freight. Ad copy should say "Australia's National and Marine Parks" in headline 1, "45 park stickers included" in headline 2 and "Designed in Perth, 1.2 L straw lid" in headline 3.
- "adventure bottle australia" and "sticker water bottle australia": cheap, brand-adjacent, but add negative "awesome maps" only if their name appears in search terms (do not negative "adventure bottle" itself, Endless Parks owns that phrase organically).
- "gifts for grey nomads", "grey nomad gifts", "gifts for road trippers", "caravan gifts": thin competition, novelty-priced rivals, and the bottle's sticker mechanic is a genuine gift story. Expect A$0.60 to A$1.30 clicks.
- "gifts for hikers australia" and "gifts for campers australia" at phrase match with a hard cap: Paddy Pallin, Macpac, BCF and Anaconda will outbid on volume, so hold these at A$1.00 and let Shopping do the work.
- "australian made t shirts" and "australian made tees" at phrase match, capped A$1.00, landing on /pages/drop-01 or the tees collection, copy leading with "Australian Made certified, hand-drawn in Perth, A$69". The query is cheap-novelty heavy (A$19 to A$32), so expect low CVR on the first click; keep it to the A$5/day non-brand budget and cut after 150 clicks without a sale.

**Avoid or hold to Shopping only:**
- "water bottle", "insulated water bottle", "stainless steel water bottle", "1.2l water bottle", "40oz water bottle", "straw water bottle": price-led, A$29 to A$80 Shopping grid, Amazon AU plus the global brands, and a A$87 bottle without a brand halo will get clicks and no sales. Do not run these as Search keywords. Add "cheap", "kmart", "amazon", "bulk", "promotional" as negatives.
- Competitor brand terms (frank green, stanley, yeti, hydro flask, owala): no evidence a sticker bottle wins these clicks; A$1.50 to A$3 CPCs are plausible given Rocking Web's A$4.12 AU average.
- "camping gifts australia" and "travel gifts australia" as Search keywords before 15 Nov: big-box owned, broad, and 2 to 3x seasonal. Reassess at BFCM with the 20 to 30% budget lift R01 proposes; let Shopping carry them.
- "sticker bottle" alone: mixed intent (sticker packs). Use only as "sticker water bottle" phrase.

**Expected CPC range for the plan:** niche park and adventure bottle terms A$0.60 to A$1.50; grey nomad and road trip gift terms A$0.60 to A$1.30; hiker and camper gift terms A$1.00 to A$2.50 in season; Australian made tee terms A$0.80 to A$1.60. Shopping clicks for the bottle should land A$0.50 to A$1.20 if the feed title reads "Australian National and Marine Parks Water Bottle 1.2L" (so it matches park and gift queries, not generic bottle queries). Benchmarks behind this: AU e-commerce average CPC A$1.82 (Rocking Web 2025), apparel Shopping CPC US$0.42 to US$0.89 (Triple Whale, FoundryCRO 2026). All are category averages, not AU drinkware data.

**Angles Endless Parks can own on Google (none of the indexed competitors use them):**
1. "Australia's National and Marine Parks" (every rival is US parks or a world map).
2. "Tick off parks with weatherproof stickers, 45 included" (Let's Wander's stickers are the recurring complaint; Judge.me 4.89 from 99 reviews backs quality).
3. "Perth designed, Aussie owned, ships from WA" versus US brands and US dollar pricing (the Meta "No more USA" winner translates directly to a responsive search ad headline: "The Aussie Alternative to US Bottles").
4. 1.2 L straw lid, 12 h hot and cold, three colours, 2 for 20% off and 3 for 30% off (automatic discounts are not "sale" hooks and may be stated as bundle pricing; confirm with Jake, see section 4).
5. For tees: "Australian Made certified" plus "hand-drawn in Perth" at A$69, where the query's incumbents are A$19 to A$32 print-on-demand with no origin claim.

**Where competition will make CPCs expensive:** anything with "water bottle" and no park qualifier; anything with "camping" or "gifts" from 15 Nov to 20 Dec (BCF, Anaconda, Snowys, Paddy Pallin, Macpac, Amazon AU all run gift guides); and competitor brand names. Shopping on generic bottle queries will also be expensive in impressions terms because the grid is A$29 to A$80 and an A$87 product gets low CTR, which drags Ad Rank.

**Feed and landing page actions this research surfaces:**
- Fix the stale A$58 and A$63 pages (and the "sold out" state on /pages/adventurebottle) before Shopping goes live, so feed price matches every reachable landing page.
- Keep Shopping ads landing on /products/ultimate-adventure-bottle, not the marketing page.
- Decide what to do about tinytreasuresdenmark.com at A$85 (see section 4).
